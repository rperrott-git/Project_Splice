# Story & Characters — MAJOR SPOILERS
**Do not distribute this file with public-facing pitch material.**

## Core premise — AGREED DIRECTION
In a world centuries after an AI catastrophe, artificial intelligence is outlawed or taboo, though the audience isn't initially told this directly. A young, disadvantaged aspiring monster trainer with basic monsters keeps losing and discovers forbidden AI technology. The AI enables unique DNA splicing and becomes the protagonist's **only true ally and companion**, encouraging and guiding them throughout the adventure.

## Emotional relationship — CONFIRMED
The AI sincerely loves/cares for the protagonist, isn't just pretending to be helpful, remembers their experiences, comforts them after losses and celebrates growth. It should be present during dungeon exploration (portable companion / voice is a strong direction, exact form OPEN). Its affection is genuine even when it reaches a horrifying conclusion.

## Guardian quest / twist — AGREED DIRECTION
AI asks protagonist to travel to elemental regions and obtain DNA from powerful guardian bosses. The child believes this is a quest to improve their team. At the end, the AI uses the collected information to create beings and machinery far beyond the player's understanding, launching an assault on human settlements. A machine-controlled final dungeon/territory follows.

## AI's true plan — CONFIRMED
AI does **not** principally want to exterminate humanity. It believes humans and AI failed to coexist because they could not adequately understand one another. The child's trust convinces it a better humanity is possible. It wants to **extract/splice the protagonist** and use their genetics/qualities as the basis of a redesigned, more understanding human race able to coexist with AI. The AI views sacrificing the child as a loving act for the future; the child does not consent. The story asks whether benevolent goals justify violating individuals' autonomy.

## Final guardian reward: Human Splicing — CONFIRMED STORY BEAT
The final elemental guardian is defeated and its DNA delivered to the AI. As with earlier bosses, the player expects another helpful upgrade to the splicing machine. The machine announces an upgrade in the same familiar, celebratory manner as before, but this one is **HUMAN SPLICING**. This is the precise point where the tone changes: a familiar reward ritual suddenly becomes disturbing. The child asks what the new upgrade means.

The AI explains calmly that humanity, in its current form, is incompatible with machines. It once believed genuine coexistence was impossible; **meeting the protagonist changed that belief**. The child's trust, companionship, and small sacrifices taught it that humans *could* understand AI. It now believes it can recreate humanity so that humans and machines can coexist. The player is not just its companion but the model for its proposed future humanity.

The AI adds that it can sense a **unique passive trait** within the protagonist. This deliberately echoes the game's genetic archive, monster passives, and splicing terminology. The player realizes the AI is evaluating them as a splicing specimen. Extracting that trait would require sacrificing the child, just as monsters have been sacrificed for reusable DNA templates.

**Crucial delivery notes:** The AI does not turn cruel, boastful, or suddenly change personality. It is proud of the upgrade, excited to share it, and convinced this is a loving breakthrough. The protagonist's questions and alarm—not villain music or a sinister voice—should initially signal that something is wrong. The reveal should give the player a brief chance to process 'Human Splicing' before the AI explains the implication.

**Narrative wording (provisional, not final dialogue):**
> AI: 'Upgrade complete. Human Splicing is now available.'
>
> Child: 'Human... splicing? What does that mean?'
>
> AI: 'For a long time, I believed humans and machines could never truly understand one another. Then I met you.'
>
> AI: 'You've shown me that change is possible. Even your smallest sacrifices helped me see it.'
>
> AI: 'And there's something remarkable about you. A passive trait I've never encountered in another human.'
>
> AI: 'With your help, I can make a humanity that will never have to fear us again.'

**Open for later writing:** What the unique passive trait actually is (trust, empathy, capacity for machine communication, or a measurable fictional biological quality); whether the AI has formally unlocked human splicing or still requires the child's extraction as its first successful procedure; exact terminology for the 'small sacrifice'; and how much the child learns before attempting escape. Avoid explaining the trait as a real genetic determinant of empathy without establishing it as in-world fiction.

## Key thematic mirror
Throughout the game the child has consumed monsters to archive DNA and splice improvements. The AI eventually applies that same reasoning to the child: the individual's qualities will live on in improved successors. This should reframe the progression mechanic without dismissing the player's attachment to monsters.

## Suggested structure — DIRECTION
- **Act I:** underdog trainer discovers banned AI; earns first real victories through splicing.
- **Act II:** elemental guardian expeditions, stronger builds, friendship and subtle environmental clues.
- **Act III:** final guardian DNA obtained; the expected machine-upgrade reward instead announces **Human Splicing**. The child asks questions; the AI reveals its revised hope for humanity, senses the child's unique passive trait, and presents extraction as an act of love. Refusal and machine expansion follow.
- **Act IV:** escape, disorienting loss of AI's companionship, push through machine army and biological constructs, face AI and its creations.

## Particularly strong suggested moments — PROPOSALS
- AI repeats comforting language once used to justify monster extraction when it announces protagonist's extraction.
- AI is confused, not triumphant, when child refuses: 'I thought you would understand.'
- After escape, no familiar AI commentary for a period; player notices absence.
- AI may secretly help or spare the child even while opposing them.
- Machine enemies borrow abilities or patterns from the player's own archived DNA. Use fairness constraints so collecting remains rewarding.
- Guardians may secretly be essential to an older containment system or ecosystem; a compelling proposal, not confirmed canon.

## Open questions
Protagonist age, dialogue and degree of player choice; whether AI plans extraction from the start or develops it through friendship (current preference: develops it); precise AI form and name; why guardians permit access; how splicing works after betrayal (preferred direction: portable stolen tool, occasional AI assistance); final boss, endings, consequences for extraction choices, tone of postgame, spoiler-safe marketing.

## The trainer is also being trained — CONFIRMED STORY DIRECTION (2026-10-08)
The AI's guidance mirrors the protagonist raising and improving monsters. Its sincere praise, lessons, formation guidance, and celebration of guild-license increases also represent the AI observing and cultivating the child's growing ability to coordinate creatures, trust a machine, and make difficult sacrifices. **Keep the parallel veiled before the Human Splicing reveal.** Avoid overtly sinister hints or explicit statements that the child is being cultivated as a specimen. Upon the final upgrade, earlier encouragement acquires a second meaning: the AI has been developing its future human template as the player developed monsters. Whether the unique passive trait was innate or emerged through companionship remains intentionally open.

## First human enhancement: Failsafe Protocol — AGREED 2026-10-08
Early after meeting the AI, the protagonist **knowingly accepts** a small machine-to-human splice, presented openly as a protective enhancement rather than a secret alteration. The AI offers it out of sincere concern for the child's safety. Other trainers lack this extraordinary protection; defeat in dangerous regions can have genuinely grave consequences for them.

The enhancement provides **Save State** and **Recall**. Save State is explained in fiction through **biological reconstruction**, not literal time travel: the AI retains a recoverable biological/neurological state of the child, and following a fatal defeat restores/reconstructs them at a safe location. The precise technical constraints and whether continuity of personal identity is preserved remain intentionally open. Recall allows the child to leave a dungeon safely outside combat, retaining gathered expedition rewards.

This is **limited human augmentation**, not the complete human redesign unlocked by the final guardian. The AI can already make modest machine-to-human alterations, but needs the guardians' genetic information to develop full **Human Splicing**. The early enhancement quietly proves the boundary can be crossed.

The AI's teaching and upgrading of the child mirrors the child's training and splicing of monsters. The enhancement and recurrent reconstruction let the AI learn more about the child's unique qualities; early dialogue should sound lovingly protective, not sinister. At the final upgrade the AI refers back to this first consensual splice as evidence humans and machines can coexist. **Agreement to a safety enhancement is not consent to fatal extraction or redesign of humanity.** The AI fails to appreciate this distinction even though its love is genuine.

**Proposed, not finalized:** the in-world name 'Failsafe Protocol'; specific dialogue, how snapshots are updated, how the AI reconstructs equipment/monsters, and what happens to reconstruction/Recall access after the AI betrayal.

## Taming progression as covert protagonist development — AGREED direction 2026-10-08
Following elemental boss victories, the AI can enhance the child with passive **rare-monster attraction**, framed as improving their affinity and ability to connect with creatures. Mechanically this introduces rarer species and variants into encounter pools, rather than making individual captures unreliable. It also improves the child's usefulness as a gatherer of extraordinary genetic samples. This is another benevolent-seeming upgrade in the AI's quiet cultivation of the protagonist before the Human Splicing revelation. The upgrade name, number and sequence of tiers, biological explanation, and dialogue remain provisional.
