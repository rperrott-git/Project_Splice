## What this is
A compact, editable source of truth from the initial design conversations. Ideas are tagged:
- **CONFIRMED** — explicitly selected or clearly agreed upon by the creator.
- **DIRECTION** — preferred approach, not a locked specification.
- **PROPOSAL** — brainstorming or example, not accepted as canon.
- **OPEN** — requires a decision or playtesting.

The design documents take precedence over casual brainstorming, but are always revisable. Do not silently turn a proposal into a confirmed feature.
